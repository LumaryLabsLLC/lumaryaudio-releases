# Lumary Audio: Downloads

Official downloads for **Lumary Audio**, a real-time voice processor, virtual microphone and tape recorder for macOS.

## Download

Get the latest version from the [**Releases**](https://github.com/LumaryLabsLLC/lumaryaudio-releases/releases/latest) page, or install it with [Homebrew](https://brew.sh):

```sh
brew install --cask lumarylabsllc/tap/lumary-audio
```

The app is signed with our Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings.

## What is Lumary Audio?

Lumary Audio cleans up your voice or instrument in real time: noise and room-echo removal, tone, de-esser, compressor and auto level, with presets and your own Audio Unit plugins. Pick **Lumary Audio** as the microphone in Zoom, Meet, Discord, OBS and more, and they hear exactly what you hear.

Its tape recorder records the mic raw, so you can give each tape a preset and plugins afterwards. Every take is stamped with time-of-day timecode, so WAV exports sync with your camera footage in Premiere, Resolve or Final Cut. Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

## Install

1. Download the latest `.dmg` from [Releases](https://github.com/LumaryLabsLLC/lumaryaudio-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **Lumary Audio** to your Applications folder.
3. Launch it and allow microphone access.
4. To use it as a microphone in other apps, press **INSTALL** next to **V-MIC** on the Live screen once (macOS asks for your administrator password), then pick **Lumary Audio** as the mic in any app.
